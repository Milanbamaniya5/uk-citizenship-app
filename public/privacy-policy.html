Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information lifeinukpractices.co.uk ("we", "our") collects and how it is used. We follow UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. If you type a name to display on a practice certificate, it stays in your browser while the page is open and is not sent to our servers. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and date and time.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads on some pages. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Where required by law, you will be asked for your consent before non-essential cookies are used. You can manage personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings and read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How we use information

We use information only to run and improve the website, keep it secure, and show ads. We do not sell personal information.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and object to certain uses of it. To make a request, email us using the details on the contact page. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

External links

Our pages link to other sites, such as GOV.UK. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.

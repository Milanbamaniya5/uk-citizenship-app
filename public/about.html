About Us

Last updated: October 2026

Life in the UK Practice is a free study website for people preparing for the Life in the UK test, which is required for indefinite leave to remain (ILR) and British citizenship applications.

What we offer

  • Practice tests: 4 sets of 24 questions, each with an explanation for the correct answer.
  • Study guides: short, plain-English summaries of history, government, law and traditions.
  • A revision plan to help you organise your study.

Why we built it

Many candidates are studying in a second language and need clear explanations, not just a list of questions and answers. We write our own questions and guides so that you understand why an answer is correct.

What we are not

We are an independent website. We are not part of the Home Office or the UK Government, and we do not issue official certificates. Our practice questions are written by us and are not the real exam questions. We cannot promise that you will pass, but studying the topics carefully gives you the best chance.

Keeping content accurate

We review our material when the official guidance changes. If you spot a mistake, please tell us on the contact page and we will correct it.

This is an independent study resource and is not affiliated with the UK Home Office or GOV.UK. Always check GOV.UK for current test rules, fees and the latest official study material.

British History Timeline for the Life in the UK Test

Last updated: October 2026

British history is the largest topic in the Life in the UK test. This timeline brings together the dates and people that appear most often so you can revise them quickly. It is a summary, so read the matching chapter in the handbook too.

Early Britain and the Romans

  • Stonehenge and other stone circles were built thousands of years ago, long before written records.
  • AD 43: Emperor Claudius leads the Roman invasion of Britain.
  • About AD 60-61: Boudicca, queen of the Iceni, leads a revolt against Roman rule.
  • From about AD 122: Hadrian's Wall is built in the north of England.
  • The Romans leave in the early 5th century.

Anglo-Saxons, Vikings and Normans

  • Angles, Saxons and Jutes settle in England after the Romans leave.
  • 597: St Augustine arrives in Kent to spread Christianity.
  • King Alfred of Wessex resists the Vikings in the 9th century.
  • 1066: William of Normandy wins the Battle of Hastings.
  • 1086: The Domesday Book records land and property.
  • 1215: King John agrees to Magna Carta at Runnymede.

Tudors and Stuarts

  • 1485: Henry VII becomes the first Tudor king after the Wars of the Roses.
  • Henry VIII breaks with the Pope in Rome, and the Church of England is created.
  • 1558-1603: Elizabeth I reigns. 1588: The Spanish Armada is defeated.
  • 1603: James VI of Scotland also becomes James I of England.
  • 1605: The Gunpowder Plot fails.
  • 1642-1651: The English Civil War. 1649: Charles I is executed.
  • 1660: The monarchy is restored under Charles II.
  • 1666: The Great Fire of London.
  • 1688-1689: The Glorious Revolution. William and Mary accept the Bill of Rights.

Union, industry and empire

  • 1707: The Act of Union joins England and Scotland.
  • The Industrial Revolution transforms factories, transport and cities in the 18th and 19th centuries.
  • 1807: The slave trade is abolished. Slavery is abolished across most of the Empire in 1833.
  • 1837-1901: Queen Victoria reigns.

The 20th century

  • 1914-1918: The First World War. 1918: Some women over 30 get the vote. Equal voting rights follow in 1928.
  • 1939-1945: The Second World War. Winston Churchill leads Britain for most of it.
  • 1948: The NHS is founded and the Empire Windrush arrives.
  • 1998: The Good Friday (Belfast) Agreement is signed in Northern Ireland.

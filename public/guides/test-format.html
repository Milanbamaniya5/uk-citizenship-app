Life in the UK Test: Format, Pass Mark and How to Book

Last updated: October 2026

The Life in the UK test is a computer-based exam taken by people applying for indefinite leave to remain (ILR) or British citizenship. It checks your knowledge of British history, traditions, laws and everyday life. This guide explains how the test works so there are no surprises on the day.

Test format

You answer 24 multiple-choice questions in 45 minutes. The pass mark is 18 correct answers, which is 75%. Some questions ask you to choose one answer from several options, and others ask you to pick the true statement from a short list. You can move between questions and change answers before you finish.

What the questions cover

Questions are based on the official handbook, Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents. Topics include the history of the UK, the system of government, the legal system, religion and customs, and public services such as the NHS. The study material can be updated, so check GOV.UK to confirm which edition is currently in use before you start revising.

Booking the test

You must book through the official GOV.UK service, never through a third-party website. You choose an approved test centre and a date, pay the fee shown at booking, and receive a confirmation. Fees and rules change from time to time, so read the current details on GOV.UK before you pay.

What to bring

Bring the same identity document you used when booking. Centres check your identity before you start, and if you do not have the right ID you may be refused entry and lose your fee. Arrive early so you have time to register calmly.

How to prepare

Read the handbook one chapter at a time rather than memorising random facts. After each chapter, test yourself with practice questions and read the explanation for every answer you get wrong. Dates, numbers and names are where most people lose marks, so write them down and revise them separately. In the final week, do full practice tests so the 24-question format feels familiar.

After the test

If you pass, you receive a pass notification letter that you need for your ILR or citizenship application. Keep it safe. If you do not pass, you can book again after a short waiting period.
